Composition and changes
-----------------------
Icons were converted to transparent PNG where needed, transparent padding
was trimmed, and instances were scaled, slightly rotated and overlapped
on a newly authored charcoal backdrop with shadows. Original icon colours
were preserved. 205 instances of 38 different source icons are included.
The 2x version is an exact nearest-neighbour enlargement for sharp edges.

The collage adaptation is provided under CC BY-SA 3.0 Unported, with the
underlying artwork credited above and its source notices preserved.
No endorsement by these projects or their authors is implied.
No uncertain-licence IRIX originals, Haiku wordmarks/leaves, or generic
distribution logos were used. Haiku is a BeOS-inspired system and is
included because this wallpaper was specifically requested for Haiku.

Exact per-icon source URLs and pinned commits are in sources.json.
