Desktop QA update — 2 November 2026

The unchanged build completed a fresh FAT-backed IRIX 5.3 installation, reached the desktop and ran uname -a, then cleanly halted and exited MAME. With original input defaults restored, a second cold launch returned to a populated desktop.

Warm restart failed with a UTLB-miss exception. Normal physical mouse behavior remains unverified; the emulated-pointer test used temporary Digital Speed settings, restored afterward. The earlier Haiku BFS/page-cache stall is unresolved; the FAT-backed trial is not an OS fix. No general stability or performance claim.
